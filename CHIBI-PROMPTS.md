## 共享提示词

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE original deity OC as a tiny hand-drawn chibi sticker. Input image 1 is a STYLE AND BRUSHWORK reference only: study ONLY the four tiny doodles in the white central area, IGNORE all phone UI, black screenshot borders, captions, logos, and existing character identities. Match that deliberately simple soft doodle language closely: an enormous rounded fluffy head takes about 70 percent of the total character height, tiny squashy bean torso and short mitten limbs, about 1.6 heads tall, tiny feet fully visible. Medium thick warm gray-brown slightly wobbly pencil-marker outlines, imperfect handmade strokes, uneven translucent pastel marker fills, subtle overlaps, very sparse flat shading. Big simple droopy half-lidded solid desaturated eyes with short black top lines, tiny dot mouth, no nose, two light rosy cheek marks. Very low detail; simple cute shapes and a few little decorative dots. A small god-specific outfit and one immediately readable prop. Front view with a slight cute head tilt. Original design, do not reproduce any character in the reference. Center a single full-body OC taking up 74-82 percent of a square canvas, plenty of clear padding, floating at most 3 tiny motif doodles. Genuinely transparent background, no white rectangle, no scenery, no frame, no text, no watermark. Avoid polished vector edges, thick black outlines, anime rendered gradients, realistic anatomy, 3D, ornate filigree, lavish costume detail, long slim bodies, and multiple characters.
